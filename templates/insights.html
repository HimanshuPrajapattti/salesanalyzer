02 · Methodology

How these findings are computed

Every signal is derived server-side from the filtered rows — fully reproducible.

Peak month

Best calendar month

Monthly revenue is aggregated with groupby('YYYY-MM'). The maximum month is compared against the mean of all months to express the lift as a percentage.

90-day momentum

Recent run rate

The average of the latest 3 months is compared with the prior 3 months. A positive delta means the business is accelerating; a negative delta flags a slowdown worth investigating.

Category leader

Top contributor

Revenue is grouped by category and sorted. The leader's share of total revenue shows how dependent performance is on a single product family.

Revenue concentration

Risk check

Threshold: 70%

The combined share of the top two categories is compared with a 70% threshold. Above it, the signal turns into a warning: losing one category would materially dent total revenue. Below it, the mix is considered balanced.
